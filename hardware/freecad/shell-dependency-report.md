## Interpretation and safe next step

The taper-related references are genuine and should eventually be replaced by fixed, shared master geometry if their shape can be preserved exactly:

- `Binder007.Support = Body.AdditiveLoft` (whole source feature).
- `Binder008.Support = Body.Sketch006` (all 16 sketch edges copied into `RearEnvelopeSeamProfile`).
- `Binder009.Support = Body.Sketch005` (all 10 sketch edges copied into `RearEnvelopeBackProfile`).

However, the graph-only counterfactual deleting just these three support-source edges removes **zero** objects from the rear active-Tip closure (331 → 331). The screw/alignment links `Binder016` / `Binder017` and `RearScrewAlignmentBinder` are a separate non-taper construction dependency. The land-transfer relation through `SmallHangerPocketBody` is the path that must be addressed first to make the rear shell independent.

The counterfactual 331 → 185 rear closure is **not** a successful CAD refactor: no geometry was modified, and it does not prove those links can be replaced while preserving the mating faces. The safe next candidate should preserve the exact saved `SmallHangerPocketBody` mating region and taper references as separately labeled, fixed master references, then redirect only disposable-copy consumers. Acceptance still requires feature recomputation, valid Tips, save/reopen, shape and mating comparisons, and a fresh cross-link report showing the unwanted direct links are gone. No candidate has passed that test yet.
